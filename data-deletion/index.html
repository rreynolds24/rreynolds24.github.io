User Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: 2 September 2026

If you have used Facebook Login to authenticate to a guest network through the FortiAuthenticator Guest Wi-Fi Facebook Login application, you may request deletion of personal information associated with that authentication.

Option 1 — Remove the application from Facebook

You can revoke the application’s future access through Facebook.

  1. Open Settings & privacy.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Locate Apps and Websites or the equivalent application-authorisation settings.
  4. Locate the FortiAuthenticator Guest Wi-Fi Facebook Login application.
  5. Select Remove and follow Facebook’s instructions.

The exact Facebook menu names may change over time.

Removing the application from Facebook prevents future use of the existing Facebook authorisation. It does not necessarily delete records that were previously created within FortiAuthenticator or retained by a guest-network operator for legitimate security, operational, or legal reasons.

Option 2 — Request deletion from the guest-authentication service

Send a deletion request to privacy@nse.expert.

Use the subject: Facebook Guest Login Data Deletion Request.

To help locate the correct record, provide only the minimum information necessary, such as:

  • the email address associated with your Facebook account, if it was shared during authentication;
  • your name;
  • the approximate date and time you used the guest network; and
  • the organisation, venue, or guest network you accessed, where known.

Do not provide your Facebook password, a Facebook access token, an application secret, authentication cookies, or any other account password.

What happens after a request

We will use the information supplied to identify records reasonably associated with the Facebook Login authentication.

Where the records are under our control and no overriding retention requirement applies, relevant Facebook-derived identity information and associated social-user records will be deleted or anonymised as appropriate.

Where another organisation operates the guest network and controls the relevant authentication or network records, the request may need to be referred to that organisation.

Some limited information may need to be retained where reasonably necessary for security incident investigation, fraud or misuse prevention, legal obligations, regulatory requirements, establishment or defence of legal claims, or maintaining evidence of the deletion request itself.

Any information retained for those purposes will not be retained merely for advertising or marketing.

Confirmation

Where reasonably practicable, confirmation will be provided after the deletion request has been processed or referred to the appropriate network operator.

Automatic expiry

FortiAuthenticator guest or social-user accounts may also be configured to expire automatically after a defined period. Expiry settings can vary between participating networks.

For additional information about how the application handles personal information, see the Privacy Policy.
