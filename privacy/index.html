Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 September 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the FortiAuthenticator Guest Wi-Fi Facebook Login integration (the “Service”). The Service enables Facebook Login to be used as an authentication method for guest wireless or captive-portal network access provided through FortiAuthenticator.

The Service has a narrow purpose: authenticating users who voluntarily choose Facebook Login when connecting to a participating guest network.

A particular organisation or venue operating a guest network may provide additional privacy notices governing its network, logging, security monitoring, acceptable-use requirements, and retention practices. Those notices apply in addition to this Privacy Policy where relevant.

2. Information processed through Facebook Login

When you choose to authenticate using Facebook, the Service may process information made available by Meta through Facebook Login. Depending on the permissions configured for the application and information available on your Facebook account, this may include:

  • an app-scoped Facebook user identifier;
  • your name;
  • basic public-profile information provided by Facebook Login; and
  • your email address, where the Facebook email permission is enabled and an email address is returned by Facebook.

The Service is intentionally configured to request the minimum Facebook information reasonably necessary for authentication.

The Service is not designed to request or access Facebook posts, private messages, friends lists, photographs or videos, Facebook Pages you administer, advertising accounts, payment information, Facebook browsing history, precise location information, or other Facebook data unrelated to guest authentication.

3. Information generated during authentication

The systems providing the guest network may also generate technical and security information as part of the authentication process. This can include authentication date and time, authentication success or failure, guest-session identifiers, network or captive-portal session information, IP and network addressing information, device or browser information routinely transmitted during web and network communication, FortiAuthenticator social-user account information, security and troubleshooting logs, and records required to enforce authentication and security policies.

The precise network logging performed by a participating organisation may vary and may be described in that organisation’s own privacy or acceptable-use notice.

4. Why information is processed

Authentication

To confirm that a user has successfully authenticated through Facebook and to establish an authenticated guest session.

Guest account administration

To create, identify, maintain, expire, or remove the corresponding FortiAuthenticator social-user or guest account where the selected configuration requires such an account.

Network access

To communicate the authentication result to the systems responsible for permitting or denying guest-network access.

Security

To protect the network, users, authentication infrastructure, and associated systems against misuse, unauthorised access, fraud, abuse, and security incidents.

Troubleshooting and service operation

To diagnose failed authentication, connectivity problems, configuration problems, or other operational issues.

Legal and compliance requirements

To retain or disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, lawful requests, or legitimate security and governance obligations.

5. What we do not do with Facebook data

Information received through Facebook Login is not used to sell personal information, build advertising profiles, deliver targeted advertising, send unrelated marketing communications, analyse your Facebook activity, publish content to Facebook, access your Facebook communications, monitor your Facebook account, or obtain Facebook information beyond the permissions required for this authentication service.

6. Sharing and disclosure

Information processed by the Service may be handled by the systems involved in providing the guest-authentication service, including FortiAuthenticator and the network infrastructure operated by the organisation providing the guest network.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to service providers supporting the relevant authentication or network infrastructure, to investigate a security incident, to prevent misuse or unauthorised access, to comply with a legal obligation or lawful request, or to establish, exercise, or defend legal rights.

Personal information received through Facebook Login is not sold to advertisers or data brokers.

7. Meta and Facebook

Facebook Login is provided by Meta. When you use Facebook Login, your interaction with Facebook and Meta’s processing of your information are also governed by Meta’s own terms, policies, and privacy practices.

This Privacy Policy governs the use of Facebook-derived information by the FortiAuthenticator guest-authentication integration after that information is made available to the Service.

8. Retention

Facebook-derived guest information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the authentication, security, operational, or legal purposes described in this Policy.

FortiAuthenticator may be configured to apply an expiry period to social guest accounts. A participating organisation may also retain authentication and security logs for a period determined by its operational, security, regulatory, or legal requirements.

Information that is no longer reasonably required should be deleted, anonymised, or allowed to expire in accordance with the applicable system and organisational retention policy. The Service is not intended to create a permanent consumer profile of a user.

9. Data deletion

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your use of Facebook Login for this Service. Detailed instructions are available at User Data Deletion Instructions.

You may also remove the application’s access from your Facebook account through Facebook’s Apps and Websites settings. Removing the application from Facebook prevents future access through that authorisation but may not automatically remove authentication or security records already held by the guest-network operator. A deletion request can be made for those records as described on the Data Deletion page, subject to any information that must lawfully or legitimately be retained.

10. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used to protect authentication information. These safeguards may include encrypted HTTPS communication, controlled administrative access, authentication and authorisation controls, network security controls, limited access to Facebook application credentials, logging and monitoring, expiration of guest accounts or sessions, and separation of authentication secrets from publicly available documentation.

No Internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Children’s information

The Service is not designed to use Facebook Login to intentionally collect additional information from children beyond the information necessary to perform the authentication transaction.

Where a participating organisation provides network access to children or students, that organisation is responsible for ensuring that its use of the Service, identity-provider options, consent mechanisms, and associated data handling comply with the laws and policies applicable to that environment.

12. International processing

Facebook, the participating network operator, and infrastructure supporting the authentication service may process information in different jurisdictions. Where required, the relevant organisation is responsible for applying appropriate privacy and data-protection safeguards to such processing.

13. Your choices

You are not required by this Service to use Facebook Login where another authentication method is made available by the network operator. If you choose Facebook Login, you may subsequently revoke the application’s Facebook access through your Facebook account settings. You may also request deletion as described in the User Data Deletion Instructions.

14. Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the authentication service, Facebook Login integration, applicable law, or data-handling practices change. The effective date shown at the beginning of the Policy will be updated when material changes are published.

15. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or requests concerning personal information associated with this application can be sent to privacy@nse.expert.

When contacting us, please do not send your Facebook password, access token, App Secret, or other authentication credentials.
